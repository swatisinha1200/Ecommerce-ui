Return Policy

Learn how to return an eligible ShopEase product.

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

1. Return Period

Eligible products may be returned within 30 days of delivery, subject to the applicable product conditions.

2. Return Conditions

To be eligible for a return, products should generally be returned in an appropriate condition with the original packaging and included accessories where applicable.

3. Items That May Not Be Eligible

  • Products marked as non-returnable.
  • Products damaged through misuse.
  • Products missing required components or accessories.
  • Products returned outside the applicable return period.

4. How to Request a Return

  1. Contact ShopEase customer service.
  2. Provide your order number.
  3. Explain the reason for the return.
  4. Follow the return instructions provided by our team.
  5. Send the product using the approved return method.

5. Return Shipping

Return shipping arrangements may vary depending on the reason for the return and the product involved.

6. Refund After Return

Once an eligible returned product has been received and reviewed, any applicable refund will be processed according to our Refund Policy.

7. Contact Us

Need help with a return? Contact ShopEase.

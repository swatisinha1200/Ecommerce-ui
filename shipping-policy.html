Shipping Policy

Everything you need to know about ShopEase shipping.

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

1. Shipping Overview

At ShopEase, we aim to deliver your orders safely and on time. Shipping options and estimated delivery times are displayed during checkout.

2. Processing Time

Orders are generally processed after payment confirmation. Processing times may vary depending on product availability, holidays, and other circumstances.

3. Shipping Options

  • Standard Shipping: Estimated delivery within 3–7 business days.
  • Express Shipping: Faster delivery may be available for selected locations.
  • Free Shipping: Available on qualifying orders according to the offer displayed at checkout.

4. Delivery Delays

Delivery times are estimates and may be affected by weather, holidays, carrier delays, incorrect addresses, or other circumstances outside our control.

5. Order Tracking

When tracking information is available, it will be provided with your order details.

6. Incorrect Shipping Address

Customers are responsible for providing accurate shipping information. Contact us as soon as possible if you need to correct your shipping address.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about your shipment, please contact us.

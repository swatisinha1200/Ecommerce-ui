Refund Policy

Information about refunds for ShopEase orders.

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

1. Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility depends on the product, reason for return, and applicable return conditions.

2. When You May Request a Refund

  • Product received damaged.
  • Incorrect product received.
  • Product has a qualifying defect.
  • Eligible product returned within the applicable return period.

3. Refund Process

After an eligible return is received and reviewed, the refund process will be initiated using the applicable payment method.

4. Refund Timing

The time required for a refund to appear in your account may vary depending on the payment provider or financial institution.

5. Non-Refundable Items

Certain products may not be eligible for refunds due to their nature or applicable restrictions. Product-specific conditions will be displayed where applicable.

6. Contact Us

For refund questions, please contact our customer service team.
